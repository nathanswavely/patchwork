Twenty-two pull requests since v0.33.0, five decision records, five
migrations (one new table, added columns, and two rewrites of stored
notification rows). No config, volume, port or Caddy changes, so this
update is the image pull alone.

## A post says whether AI made it

Events, patches, proposals and notices now ask whether AI made them:
`none`, `assisted` or `generated`, with nothing preselected and NULL for
unanswered. Event and patch images are asked separately. Only `generated`
is ever shown on the post. Imported events stay unanswered and show where
they came from instead
(`docs/adr/2026-10-20-a-post-says-whether-ai-made-it.md`, #456).

- **The instance switch** is `ai_disclosure`, at Admin → Settings → Quilt:
  `off`, `optional` (the default) or `required`. Under `required` a new
  post without an answer is refused with the field named; an edit that
  leaves it out keeps the stored answer.
- **A patch whose work is made with AI** can say so (`ai_work`), and a
  person can hide those patches from the quilt, the map and the events
  feed under Account settings. It never hides them from My Quilt, and
  there is no instance-wide version of the filter.
- **Reports** gain the reason "unlabelled AI", and moderators can label a
  post or its image `generated`.

## The site says where AI is

A public page at **/ai**, linked from the footer beside Privacy
(`docs/adr/2026-10-20-the-site-says-where-ai-is.md`, #454, #440). The
project's part ships in the binary and no instance can edit it: nothing on
the site sends anything to a model, these are the machines the server
talks to, these are the AI crawlers it refuses, and this is how much of
the writing a person did, with `copy/REPORT.md` as the record. Its claims
are held by tests, so a new outbound connection or a model SDK fails the
build until the page says so.

**The stewards' part is empty until you write it**, at Admin → Settings →
Legal under "The AI page: your part". Until then the page says the stewards have
not added anything.

The About page's "open source" link pointed at an unrelated project that
shares Patchwork's Go module path; it now points at this repository, and
two copy-ledger attributions were corrected in both directions (#455).
SlopChop is pinned at 0.4.1 (#453).

## Notifications

- **On a desktop the bell opens a dropdown** of the latest notifications,
  with Mark all read, a way through to the full page and a link to
  notification settings. It replaces a side panel that slid in from the
  left. A phone still goes straight to the page (#460).
- **Each row names its sender** above the title: the patch, a followed
  patch on another quilt and its host, "Admin" for an instance queue, or
  nothing for what the quilt says about you. The type icons and the tinted
  unread rows are gone; unread is a dot. The time sits under the row in
  words.
- **The page filters by All and Unread.** The type chips missed half the
  types. It also links to notification settings now.
- **Proposal and comment notifications carry an excerpt**, not the whole
  proposal or comment, as notices already did.
- **Membership notices say who** joined, asked or was invited, and a join
  request opens the queue rather than the roster (#430).
- **An archived patch leaves the bell.** Its notifications are hidden and
  stop counting, and come back if it is restored (#426).

## The quilt and the map

- **Filter chips sit on the canvas's top edge**, one row of the most-used
  tags and "N more", which grows into a card with a find field. Quilt/Map
  and zoom moved to the bottom-right (#457).
- **A tile wears one motif.** Corner marks are bare glyphs with a shadow
  only where the fabric needs one, and a labelled tile's motif moves into
  its name badge (#459).
- **A phone shows the quilt's name** in the header, fading out when it has
  to give way (#458).
- **A patch can say it is a nonprofit.** It is a fact on the patch, set by
  its admins or whoever suggested the listing, shown as a pill. It is not
  a tag, so it never moves a patch on the quilt
  (`docs/adr/2026-10-25-nonprofit-is-a-fact-not-a-tag.md`, #449).

## Governance

- **An interim admin is asked first.** When inactivity empties a
  longest-tenure patch, its three longest-standing members are asked
  whether they will step in, and nothing changes unless one accepts. They
  used to be installed and told afterwards
  (`docs/adr/2026-10-19-an-interim-admin-is-asked-first.md`, #427).
- **Nominations take effect on admin-decides patches**, through every door
  the maintainer decides by
  (`docs/adr/2026-10-18-a-nomination-takes-effect-where-the-decision-lands.md`,
  #416).
- **A settled proposal's Changes tab shows what it changed**, rather than
  "No changes" once the change had applied (#433).
- **The rules editor** keeps your changes when you go back from review
  (#432), and its suggested summary names every field changed, not five of
  eighteen (#425).
- **Fixes from the governance simulation:** an election is never shown as
  a direct change, a closed proposal says when it closed, opening an
  election is on the record, and several other proposal and governance
  page corrections (#431, #430, #426).
- **Comment authors can edit and delete their own comments**, and the
  nominee picker reaches past the first hundred members (#413).
- **Four places that said something untrue were corrected**, among them
  an admin cap the product does not enforce (#424). Six shell and copy
  findings were fixed, among them notification bodies that clipped on a
  desktop and a welcome redirect that lost the page asked for (#429).

## Upgrading

**Take a backup first**, then:

```sh
docker compose pull patchwork && docker compose up -d
```

Five migrations run:

- `interim_admin_offers`, a new table;
- `join_request_links`, which rewrites stored join-request notification
  links to point at the queue;
- `nonprofit` on `nodes`;
- the AI disclosure columns on `events`, `nodes`, `proposals`, `notices`
  and `users`;
- `node_id` on `notifications`, filled from each row's link.

No `git pull` is needed; nothing outside the image changed.

Once it is up, write your part of the AI page at Admin → Settings →
Legal, and check that the AI disclosure setting at Admin → Settings →
Quilt is the one your quilt wants. It ships as optional.

**Rolling back to v0.33.0 is not clean.** Nothing in the data is lost, but
the older binary refuses to start against a database whose
`schema_migrations` names a migration it does not know. To go back,
restore the backup taken above rather than starting the older image
against this database.
