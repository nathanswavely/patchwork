Three pull requests since v0.32.0, one amended decision record, one
migration (two added columns, nothing rewritten). No config, volume, port
or Caddy changes, so this update is the image pull alone.

## An intro can be a message on a link

v0.32.0 shipped the intro, and the reference instance's first pass at its
backlog found the obvious hole: many of the organizations a young quilt
lists have no public email at all and live on Instagram. A contact is now a
place to reach someone, in two shapes that may both be on file.

- **Patch Settings → Intro** is "Where to reach them": an email address, a
  link to message them on, or both. The patch's own links are offered as
  one-click choices, since the Instagram on the listing is usually the
  door. A bare handle is refused rather than guessed into a network; it has
  to be a full link.
- **A message on a link** renders a short opener of its own (the first two
  lines are all a message preview shows), the same calendar paragraph the
  probe picked, no subject and no sign-off. It is always copied out and
  sent by hand, then recorded against the link, under the same
  one-per-destination rule. The opener is its own field on the Outreach
  Template tab (`dm_opener`).
- **It never carries the one-click claim link.** A mailbox at the
  verification domain is proof of the domain (docs/adr/030); a social
  account proves nothing about it. A message carries the ordinary claim
  page, and the Intro section says so.
- **Email is the default** when both are on file, since it is the channel
  that can carry the shortcut, and the admin can switch. Copy-and-record is
  now offered for email too, for an admin who prefers their own mail
  client to the relay.
- The on/off switch on the Outreach page is a Liquid Textile switch beside
  its words rather than a checkbox floating above them.

`POST /api/v1/nodes/{slug}/intro/{preview,send}` take `channel: email | dm`;
`PUT /api/v1/nodes/{slug}/contact` takes `email` and `url`, each optional.
`nodes.contact_url` and `intro_sends.channel` are the new columns, and both
stay behind at the seamrip boundary like `contact_email`. The amendment is
at the end of `docs/adr/2026-10-16-a-page-made-for-someone-tells-them-so.md`
(#422).

## The map opens where the markers are

A map that mounted before its container had a width spent its opening fit
there: Leaflet does not refuse a fit with no room, the padded size goes
negative, and the view lands at the zoom ceiling off every marker, over
tiles that come back 400. The opening fit now waits for room and retries
when it arrives, the GL basemap's load window is counted in frames the
page actually drew rather than wall-clock time (a hidden tab draws none),
and the raster fallback stops asking OpenStreetMap for a zoom level it
does not serve (#418). This predates v0.32.0; it only looked new.

## Upgrading

**Take a backup first**, then:

```sh
docker compose pull patchwork && docker compose up -d
```

One migration runs: `contact_url` on `nodes` and `channel` on
`intro_sends`, both with defaults. No `git pull` is needed for this one;
nothing outside the image changed.

**Rolling back to v0.32.0 is not clean**, for the reason v0.32.0's notes
gave: nothing in the data is lost, but the older binary refuses to start
against a database whose `schema_migrations` names a migration it does not
know. To go back, restore the backup taken above rather than starting the
older image against this database.
