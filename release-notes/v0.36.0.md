Seven pull requests since v0.35.2, nine decision records, and two
migrations. Most of this release comes from the governance simulation:
seventeen findings, decided on 2026-10-20 and built in three batches. No
config, volume, port or Caddy changes, so this update is the image pull
alone. Clients have two things to handle; see "For client developers".

## Governance

- **A supermajority passes at exactly two thirds.** A 2–1, 4–2 or 6–3 vote
  was recorded as rejected, though every template and the vote panel say
  "at least two thirds". Votes settled before this release keep their
  outcome (#484).
- **A member can stand for a vacant seat.** On an elected patch, a vacant
  chair that is not in a running contest shows "Stand for this seat" to its
  members. Standing raises the same nomination the members ratify when an
  admin puts somebody forward, and the chair keeps its remaining term.
  Putting somebody else forward is still an admin's (#477).
- **Your own candidacy no longer asks for your vote.** "N proposals need
  your vote" on the governance hub and the Dashboard skips a proposal about
  you, unless nobody else could vote on it (#481).
- **A member can file the minutes.** On a patch that decides anything
  elsewhere, any active member may add a governance document. It is born
  members-only. Publishing it, and recording leadership changes or adopted
  texts, stay with admins (#477).
- **A patch that meets elsewhere says where.** The governance rules gain a
  short note, for example "First Tuesday of the month, 7pm, the church
  hall", and the Governance tab shows it in place of the bare "outside
  Patchwork" (#477).
- **A record of a decision made elsewhere names who stood down**, beside who
  it put in place. An admin can mark a record **entered in error**: it stays
  on the Record, crossed out, and stops counting. Nothing is deleted. Marking
  the record in force puts the previous one back in force (#477).
- **Adding or removing a seat no longer rings every member's bell.** The
  change goes on the governance Record with the council's new size. A term
  end moving still notifies (#477).

## Templates and new patches

- **A template gives a new patch the documents its preview lists.** The
  Formal preview promised six documents and the patch got one, because the
  others were written to the charter repository only. New patches now get
  every listed document as a members-only charter. Patches created before
  this release keep what they have (#475).
- **The template documents were checked against the code** and corrected
  where they described something Patchwork does not do. For example, they
  said ranked-choice where elections use approval voting, and described
  nomination windows, recall petitions and suspensions that do not exist.
  The full list is in
  `docs/adr/2026-11-11-a-template-gives-the-patch-what-its-preview-lists.md`.
  Existing charters are unchanged (#475).
- **The template cards say who decides and how**, drop the group sizes, and
  each says decisions can be made at a meeting instead. The preview shows
  quorum and the voting window only on templates that vote. This amends
  docs/adr/104 (#475).
- **Creating a patch asks its timezone**, preselecting the creator's own and
  offering the quilt's beside it. Left empty, the patch follows the quilt as
  before (#475).

## Members, the Dashboard and the quilt

- **Answering a join request clears its notification** for every admin it
  reached, on approve, reject, or the requester withdrawing. A follower
  asking to become a member on an approval-required patch now sends a
  request, where it was announced as "New member joined" (#476).
- **An invitation records who sent it.** The invitee sees "June Okafor
  invited you to join" on the patch and their Dashboard. The patch's admins
  see who invited each person, and the Members tab now lists outstanding
  invitations to them (#476).
- **Settings → Members is now "Roles and requests"**, and the Members tab
  links to it for admins. The address is unchanged (#476).
- **The Dashboard splits "Needs you" from "Coming up".** Invitations,
  requests and votes you owe come first, then your own requests waiting on
  an answer, then events on your patches. Every line names its patch rather
  than showing its address (#476).
- **The role chip's menu** offers Settings and Notifications, with Leave
  last (#476).
- **My Patches** reads "Contact: not shared" or "Contact: 2 shared", in place
  of "Not reachable" (#476).
- **The star on a tile says what it means**, on hover and to screen readers:
  "You're an admin here", "You're a member here" or "You follow this patch"
  (#476).

## Smaller things

- Page titles and link previews join the page's name to the quilt's with a
  pipe instead of a dash, and the privacy and terms pages drop "The fine
  print" (#473).
- Go 1.26.11, for the `net/http` advisory GO-2026-6859 (#476, #478).

## For client developers

**Two changes can make an existing client misread something.** Both appear
only after somebody uses the new feature, which is why `client_breaking` is
true.

1. **Struck attestations stay in the list.** `GET
   /api/v1/nodes/{slug}/attestations` returns records an admin has marked
   entered in error, with `entered_in_error: true`, `struck_at` and
   `struck_by_name`. Such a record is never in force, and a correction of
   it never counts as one. A client that works out which record is in force
   by following `supersedes_id` must skip struck records, or it will show
   the wrong council.
2. **The governance rules file gains `venue_note`.** Rules changes replace
   the whole `governance-rules.json`, so a client that builds a new rules
   file from fields it knows, rather than from what it loaded, will clear
   the note. Carry over keys you don't recognise. A non-empty note is refused
   where nothing is decided elsewhere, and is limited to 200 characters.

Additive:

- `POST /api/v1/nodes/{slug}/attestations/{id}/strike` (admin, step-up):
  marks a record entered in error.
- Attestations: each entry gains `stood_down` beside `names`, on reads and
  on create. Naming the same person on both sides is a 400. `stood_down`
  names carry no role.
- Proposals: a member may create a membership proposal naming themselves
  for a vacant seat on an elected patch. It was refused before.
- Governance documents: an active member may create one on a patch that
  decides anything elsewhere. It is always members-only; asking for
  `public` is a 403.
- `needs_vote` on the governance overview and `awaiting_your_vote` on the
  proposals list no longer count a proposal about the caller. Same fields,
  same shape.
- `POST /api/v1/nodes` accepts `timezone`, validated as Patch Settings
  validates it; empty means follow the quilt.
- The template preview's documents now arrive in a fixed order, each with a
  `title`, and match what a new patch receives.
- Invitations carry `invited_by` (id, username, display name) in the
  admins' `invited` list on the patch's members payload, in
  `GET /api/v1/users/me/invitations`, and in the personal export. It is
  blank for an invitation made before this release.
- A `membership.request` notification is deleted when its request is
  answered. A client holding one should treat a 404 on it as answered.

## Upgrading

**Take a backup first**, then:

```sh
docker compose pull patchwork && docker compose up -d
```

Two migrations run:

- `notifications.membership_id` and `memberships.invited_by`, two nullable
  columns. The first migration also deletes join-request notifications for
  requests that are already answered, which cannot be undone.
- `attestation_names.effect` (default `seated`), `attestations.struck_at`
  and `attestations.struck_by`, three added columns.

**Rolling back to v0.35.2 is not clean.** The older binary refuses to start
against a database whose `schema_migrations` names a migration it does not
know. To go back, restore the backup taken above.

No `git pull` is needed; nothing outside the image changed.
