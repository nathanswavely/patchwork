Out of scope, on purpose:

- `docs/adr/`: Engineering records: the project's reasoning, not its voice. Rewriting 50k words of decision history by hand would buy nothing.
- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`: The Contributor Covenant, a standard text authored by neither a person here nor a model. Rewriting it to sound like us would defeat the point of adopting it.
- `web/src/vendor/`: Liquid Textile, vendored: copied in by scripts/sync-lt.sh and never edited here. Its own strings (a combobox's empty list, a failed copy) are reviewed in Liquid Textile, which is where a fix would go.
- `internal/**/*.go`: Only the files listed in include ship prose, and every handler is read for its {"error":"..."} bodies alone, so log lines and SQL stay out.
- Frozen constants (`legacyLiningOriginal`, `legacyLiningHumanized`): matched byte-for-byte by the program and never offered for rewriting.
